Choosing the platform

Google Ads vs. Meta Ads: which is better for your business?

There is no single best platform for every business: the choice depends on the campaign objective. In most cases, Google Ads and Meta Ads complement each other by reaching customers at different stages of the decision.

Comparison between Google Ads and Meta Ads
CriterionGoogle AdsMeta Ads (Facebook and Instagram)
How the customer reaches the adThey search Google for a solution, product or service.They see the ad while browsing Facebook or Instagram.
Purchase intentUsually closer to a decision because it starts with an active search.Can create interest before the person actively searches.
Best forCapturing existing demand, calls, directions, quotes and contacts.Reaching audiences by interests, behavior, location and profile.
Ad formatsSearch, Maps, display, video and other available formats.Images, videos, carousels, stories, reels and other social formats.
How billing worksIt depends on the campaign and bidding strategy; billing is commonly related to clicks, impressions or conversions.It depends on the objective and auction; billing may be related to impressions, clicks or actions.
Speed of responseCan generate visits and contacts once ads are approved, with no guarantee of results.Can generate reach and engagement quickly, while contacts depend on the offer, audience and creative.
Restaurant or bar exampleShowing up for “bar near me”, “pizza buffet today” or “restaurant open now”.Promoting happy hour, an event or a new dish to people in the area with a relevant profile.

Using only one platform may make sense when the budget or objective is very specific. Using both can broaden coverage: Google Ads captures existing demand and Meta Ads helps create interest and recall.

Management decision

Should you run ads yourself or hire a paid traffic manager?

Running ads yourself can reduce management costs, but it requires time to learn, monitor data and fix mistakes. Professional management makes sense when the owner needs strategy, measurement and optimization without taking on the daily operation.

Comparison between running ads yourself and hiring professional management
CriterionDo it yourselfHire professional management
Owner's timeThe owner researches, sets up, monitors and adjusts campaigns.The owner helps with business decisions while the manager handles the operation.
Learning curveYou need to learn platforms, metrics, targeting and policies.The manager provides the technical knowledge and explains decisions clearly.
Risk of wasted budgetIt can be higher when setup or targeting is incorrect.It may be reduced through planning, tracking and reviews, but never eliminated.
Tracking and measurementIt depends on the owner's knowledge and availability.It includes setup and review of indicators based on the agreed objective.
Continuous optimizationIt happens when the owner has time to analyze data.It is part of the management routine, with adjustments to audiences, ads and budget.
CostAd spend plus the owner's time to operate the account.Ad spend plus the agreed management fee.
Who it suitsBusinesses with available time, a simple operation and willingness to learn.Businesses that want to delegate operations and make data-informed decisions.

Doing it yourself can still make sense when the budget is small, the campaign is simple and the owner has time to study and monitor the account regularly.

Meta Ads

Should you boost a post or run a professional ad campaign?

Boosting a post is a simple way to distribute an existing publication more widely. A professional ad campaign gives you more control over the objective, audience, tracking, testing and optimization.

Comparison between boosting a post and running a professional campaign
CriterionBoosting a postProfessional campaign
ObjectiveIncrease reach or engagement for a publication.Generate a defined action, such as a contact, visit, lead or sale.
Audience controlSimpler audience and placement options.Targeting, exclusions, location, audiences and placements configured for the strategy.
Conversion trackingIt may be limited or depend on the existing setup.It can use Pixel, events and other tracking tools according to the objective and applicable consent.
A/B testingLimited control to compare audience and creative variations.Allows testing ads, offers, audiences and pages when there is enough data to measure.
OptimizationUsually depends on the platform's simplified options.Uses campaign data to adjust budget, ads, audiences and next tests.
Expected outcomeMore distribution and interaction, with no guarantee of contacts or sales.More control over the desired action, with no guarantee of results.
When to use eachWhen you want to promote a specific publication quickly.When there is a commercial goal and you need to track the path to conversion.
Planning and execution

How long does it take to launch a paid traffic campaign and start seeing results?

The campaign usually goes live within 3 to 5 business days after the briefing and access are provided. The first useful data for optimization typically appears after about 2 weeks, while more stable and predictable performance usually requires approximately 3 months of data collection and optimization. These are market benchmarks, not a guarantee of results.

  1. 1

    Diagnosis and briefing

    Understanding the business, offer, audience, location and objective.

  2. 2

    Account, Pixel and tracking setup

    Organizing accounts and the events needed to measure relevant actions.

  3. 3

    Ad creation and platform approval

    Producing copy, creatives and settings, followed by platform review.

  4. 4

    Campaign goes live

    Ads begin competing for impressions according to budget, audience and auction conditions.

  5. 5

    Learning and data collection

    The campaign gathers data about reach, clicks, contacts and costs.

  6. 6

    First optimization

    The data guides adjustments to audiences, ads, keywords, budget or offer.

  7. 7

    Weekly routine

    The account is monitored and next decisions are based on what was observed.

Timing varies according to budget, competition, offer, landing page and response speed. Platform approval and data quality can also change the campaign's pace.
