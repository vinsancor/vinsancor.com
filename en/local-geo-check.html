What is GEO?

GEO means Generative Engine Optimization — optimizing a digital presence for search engines and experiences powered by artificial intelligence. In practice, the goal is to help search systems find, understand and correctly contextualize information about a company, its services, location, differentiators and authority.

It's not about manipulating the AI.

It's about building a digital presence that makes sense to people, search engines and generative systems.

What does GEO Local Check™ analyze?

Google Business Profile

Local SEO
