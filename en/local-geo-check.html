What is GEO?

GEO means Generative Engine Optimization — digital presence optimization for artificial intelligence-based search engines and experiments. In practice, the goal is to help search systems find, understand and correctly contextualize information about a company, its services, its location, its differentials and its authority.

It's not about manipulating the AI.

It's about building a digital presence that makes sense to people, search engines and generative systems.

What does GEO Local CheckTM analyze?

Repute
