BEFORE AND AFTER

A clearer message changes how an offer is understood

See how copywriting turns a generic sentence into a specific, useful message that guides the reader toward the next step.

Before · generic copy

“We offer quality services for you. Get in touch.”

Reader impact: it does not explain the benefit, who the service is for or what happens after getting in touch.

After · copywriting applied

“Save time during your week with healthy meals prepared for you. View the menu and order on WhatsApp.”

Reader impact: it presents a concrete benefit, gives context to the offer and makes the next step clear.
